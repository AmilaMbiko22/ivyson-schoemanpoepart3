About Ivyson & Schoeman

Our history, mission and the people working to keep your household safe.

Our History

Ivyson & Schoeman Home Security was formed through a partnership between Samuel Ivyson and Du-Plessis Schoeman, who came together to combat and thwart criminal activity directed at personal households. Since then, the organisation has grown into a trusted supplier and installer of home security equipment.

Our Mission

To provide homeowners with a cheap, reliable and trustworthy assortment of home protection equipment and installation services, building an accessible digital presence that turns homeowners into satisfied clients.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted home security partner for middle-class families, helping every household feel safe through affordable technology and honest advice.

Our Goals

  • Grow quotation requests submitted online by 25%
  • Clearly communicate equipment specs, pricing and installation
  • Boost local search traffic with educational safety content
